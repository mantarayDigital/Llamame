What Makes Us Different

Your AI scheduling
co-pilot

💡

Energy-Aware Scheduling

Tell Llamame when you're a morning person or night owl. It'll prioritize deep-work calls during your peak hours and save admin calls for low-energy slots.

🔥

Meeting Fatigue Protection

Auto-detects when you're overbooked and suggests rescheduling non-urgent meetings. Prevents burnout before it happens.

🎓

Client Memory

Remembers every interaction with each client. Notes, preferences, past meeting summaries — all surfaced automatically before your next call.

🚀

Smart Rescheduling

When a client cancels, Llamame finds the next best time for both parties and sends a one-click reschedule link. Zero back-and-forth.

✨ Llamame AI
Hey, I noticed you have 6 meetings tomorrow with zero breaks. Want me to suggest some buffer time?
Yes please, and move the non-urgent ones to Thursday
✨ Llamame AI
Done! I moved 2 check-in calls to Thursday afternoon (their preferred time anyway). Tomorrow now has 30-min buffers between deep-work sessions. Your energy score just went from 🔴 to 🟢
What about the strategy call with Alex?
✨ Llamame AI
Alex prefers mornings and your energy peaks at 10 AM. I kept it at 10:00 - 11:00 AM. Here's a quick brief: Last meeting you discussed Q1 roadmap. Alex mentioned wanting to revisit budget allocation.
